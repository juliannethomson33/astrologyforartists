Opening freewrite
To begin, please describe your creative process from start to finish.
more questions to consider for this free write
  • What do you do to begin working?
  • What happens while you're working?
  • How do you complete working?
  • Is your process flowing with other areas of your life — eating, other jobs, family — or is it distinct?
  • What tools do you use in your process, physically?
  • Where are you, where do you go physically to do your creative work?
  • Where do you go emotionally to do your creative work?
  • Be as detailed as possible from your own memory recall. It doesn't have to be definitive — you can always come back and rewrite later.
Your writing saves automatically as you type and will still be here if you close and return to this page.

Reference
Planets & Points
Sun—How you shine; you are always creating this way
Moon—How you nurture yourself; how you create from a healthy place
Mercury—How you rationalize, write about, and communicate your creative process
Venus—How your work is accessible by others; how your work is made
Mars—How you physically act to make your creative work
Jupiter—How you understand your own creative work and sell your creative process
Saturn—How you stabilize your physical creative environment
Uranus—How you evolve creatively
Neptune—How you're connected to unknowable truth; how you receive creative inspiration
Pluto—How you fundamentally change throughout your creative process
South Node—How you can already do it
North Node—How you want to do it
AC—How you show up in any creative space
IC—How you're most connected to yourself in your process
DC—How you see others and your creative environment
MC—How you are seen by others in your creative world
Signs
Aries—Immediately through instinctive action… live action, cutting/fire, physical presence
Taurus—Slowly through touch, taste, smell, hearing, sight… sensual, luxurious material focus, ripe
Gemini—Easily through curiosity, conversation and data… writing, collage, printmaking, bookbinding, fonts, comics
Cancer—Lovingly through emotion and nurturing… "body of work", feeling-focused, family-focused, home/ancestry-focused
Leo—Warmly through creativity, joy and expression… colorful, charisma, pride of work
Virgo—Meticulously through devotion and analysis… graphic design, calligraphy, realism
Libra—Socially through relationships and diplomacy… classical beauty, compositional balance
Scorpio—Strategically through darkness and power… occult/underworld/psychic themes, extreme sensitivity and attunement
Sagittarius—Excitedly through freedom and understanding… large-scale, strong meaning, exploration of higher concepts through simple nature-based process, international interest
Capricorn—Structurally through focus and sustainable processes… strong work ethic and heavy concepts/themes that pervade the societal times
Aquarius—Intellectually through new ideas and niche interests… group interest, intellectual influence, writing, sci-fi, horizons, other worlds
Pisces—Openly through imagination and flow… dream influence, mystical themes, infinity, water-based
